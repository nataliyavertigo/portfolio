Product Design Approach

1

Understand

I evaluate the existing experience through research, customer behavior, analytics, and stakeholder context to understand where friction exists and what may be limiting the product.

2

Define

From there, insights are shaped into a focused product direction that balances customer needs, business priorities, and technical realities.

3

Design

I explore and shape solutions from early concepts through detailed interaction and interface design, testing different approaches and challenging assumptions along the way. Working closely with product, engineering, and cross-functional partners, I turn strategy into intuitive, scalable experiences grounded in user needs, business goals, and the realities of the product.

4

Deliver & Iterate

I drive the work through testing, implementation, and QA, working with engineering to resolve issues, protect the experience, and identify further opportunities for improvement.
